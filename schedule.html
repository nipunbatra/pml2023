Lecture library

Every lecture of ES 661 Probabilistic machine learning, August to November 2023, with its slides, notebooks, cheat sheet and recording.

Each row is one lecture, grouped by part of the course. The course is built on one equation, Bayes' rule for the parameters of a model, and moves from point estimates (maximum likelihood, MAP) to whole posteriors, then to three ways of approximating a posterior: Laplace, sampling and variational inference. Slides and notebooks are from pml-teaching; some notebooks are also on the notebooks page. Every lecture recording is in the YouTube playlist. Three-minute summary videos, each on one idea, are being added and will appear on the rows they belong to.

Slides: lecture slides (PDF) · Notebooks: worked code · Cheat sheet: a two-page A4 summary (PDF) · 3-min summary: a three-minute video on one idea; where a row has several, point at a number to see its title · Recording: the full lecture on YouTube · Reading: articles and tutorials for going further.
